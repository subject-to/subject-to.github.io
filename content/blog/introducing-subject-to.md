<!--Every significant business decision—how much inventory to stock, what price to set, when reorder—is made sequentially under uncertainty with new information arriving after every decision. These are **sequential decision problems**. 

Traditionally, the tools organizations reach for to solve them are either: (a) borrowed from communities that each solve a narrow slice of a sequential decision problem in isolation—operations research, machine learning, reinforcement learning, stochastic programming—each with its own language and assumptions, or (b) reduced to heuristics and or expert gut instinct because no toolkit and framework is accessible to practitioners.

`Subject-To` provides a toolkit and standardized framework for modeling and solving **any** sequential decision problem. The framework focuses on a _model first, then solve_ mentality resulting in a two layer framework.

1. **Modeling**. Develop models of your business process (system).
2. **Policy design**. Design and identify the right type of decision rule (policy) for the problem.

The generality of our framework allows you to separate the process of developing the model from designing the policy, thereby enabling you to easily compose and compare a wide variety of models and policies.

The `Subject-To` framework can be used for a variety of applications

- Inventory (storage).
- Resource allocation.
- Shortest path.
- Pricing.-->
