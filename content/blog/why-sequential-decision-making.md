A sequential decision problem, simply stated, consists of the sequence

$$\text{Information} \rightarrow \text{Decision} \rightarrow \text{Information}$$

Whenever we make a decision, we incur costs or earn rewards. The challenge lies in how to represent the information that will arrive in the future, and how to make decisions, both now and in the future.

But we first need to understand what method is being used to make a decision. We will use the term **policy** to

Every policy method is proposed from the fields of Reinforcement Learning, Optimal Control, Operations Research, etc. fall into the four following policy classes

1. Policy function approximation (PFA).
2. Cost function approximation (CFA).
3. Value function approximation (VFA).
4. Direct lookahead approximation (DLA).
